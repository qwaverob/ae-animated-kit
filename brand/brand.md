# A&E Brothers Roofing: brand and rules

The engine already applies the colors, fonts, art style and layout. You only write words.

## Look (already built in)
- Navy `#13294B` background, white text, orange `#F47B20` on 1 to 3 key words (wrap them in `**like this**`).
- Fonts: Poppins (headlines and text), Anton (captions and keyword stamps). Both bundled with an open license.
- One flat illustration style: same house, same palms, same props, 6 px dark outline on everything.
- Logo: a text placeholder "A&E Brothers Roofing" with an orange roof mark. **Check with A&E:** official logo file.

## Audience
South Florida homeowners, 35 to 70, many bilingual. They worry about hurricanes, leaks, insurance and cost. Plain words, no roofing jargon.

## Rules (the build fails on the first three)
1. Never use long dashes. Periods or commas.
2. Any mention of "$1,000 off" must show **"Reroof only, minimum 20 squares."** on the same slide or scene.
3. Slide headline 12 words max. Line under it 15 words max.
4. One call to action per post. Pick one: Save this. Comment REFER. DM us a photo. Book a free inspection, link in bio.
5. Referral CTA: "Comment REFER and we'll send you the link." Link: refer.aebrothersroofing.com
6. Insurance topics: add `"disclaimer": "General tips, not legal or insurance advice."` on the last slide or end card.
7. Never invent prices, warranties, wind ratings, reviews, customer stories or time frames.
8. No music inside the files. Add a sound in Instagram if you want.
9. No hashtags or emojis on slides. They go in the caption.
10. No real people, no cartoon characters from shows or brands, no cloned voices.
11. Never tell people to climb on a roof. "From the ground" or "Send us a photo."

## Check with A&E (add a line to the end of your reply when a post uses one)
- Official logo file and brand colors.
- Is the roof inspection free, and what does it include?
- Free yearly inspection with a reroof: exact wording and how many years.
- Does the inspection include documenting storm damage for insurance?
- "DM us a photo": who answers and how fast?
- OK with "General tips, not legal or insurance advice"?
- Material cost and lifespan wording (shingle vs tile vs metal).
- OK to add a Spanish line to every caption?
